# NY — 2026 review

Version: US-NY-2026-v1. Reviewed September 21, 2026. Applies only to the stated 2026 nonresident regular-W-2 scope. Primary-source review is not professional legal approval.

## Filing exposure

New York-source income creates exposure; NY adjusted gross income and standard deduction determine filing.

## Withholding and allocation

14-day administrative relief is conditional; expecting more than 14 days can require withholding from the first day.

Annual workday allocation subject to assigned-office and convenience sourcing review.

Review withholding on all New York wages for the year, including earlier days.

## Open questions / limits

- NY-assigned offices require convenience-of-employer analysis.
- Training and special occupations are outside this prototype.
- Yonkers/local obligations and final filing tests require review.

Do not apply this version to 2027. Final return requirements, unusual compensation and occupational exceptions require separate review.
