# CO — 2026 review

Version: US-CO-2026-v1. Reviewed September 21, 2026. Applies only to the stated 2026 nonresident regular-W-2 scope. Primary-source review is not professional legal approval.

## Filing exposure

Colorado-source income plus a federal filing requirement or Colorado tax liability.

## Withholding and allocation

Wages for Colorado services enter withholding treatment.

Pay-period workday ratio; Colorado counts a day when the majority of services occur there.

Apply Colorado work-location allocation to the pay period.

## Open questions / limits

- Travel to Colorado has special day-count treatment; travel-service sessions require review.
- Local occupational taxes are not calculated.

Do not apply this version to 2027. Final return requirements, unusual compensation and occupational exceptions require separate review.
