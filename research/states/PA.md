# PA — 2026 review

Version: US-PA-2026-v1. Reviewed September 21, 2026. Applies only to the stated 2026 nonresident regular-W-2 scope. Primary-source review is not professional legal approval.

## Filing exposure

More than $33 of PA taxable income under the regular-wages-only scope; reciprocity can exempt compensation.

## Withholding and allocation

PA service wages enter withholding unless reciprocal-state requirements are satisfied.

Workday allocation for nonresident salary with adequate records; assigned-office sourcing and localities require review.

Apply PA wage allocation and review local EIT/LST and residency certification.
